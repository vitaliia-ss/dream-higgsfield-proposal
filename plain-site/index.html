Partnership proposal

Dream × Higgsfield

Every dream is a story to be told.

Dream helps people start the business they have always dreamed of. Higgsfield is the studio where those dreams are turned into cinematic images, video and characters. Here are three ways to work together.

↓ See the three ideas

Why this fits

Both of us turn an idea into something real.

Dream helps people start the business they have always dreamed of. Simon Squibb, the visionary behind Dream, makes inspiring social media content about people chasing their dreams and has a following of 28 million.

Higgsfield is an all-in-one AI creative studio for cinematic images, video and characters, with over 25 million users. Its mission is to make professional video and image creation accessible to anyone with an idea.

Together

Higgsfield lets anyone turn a dream into something they can see, and Dream gives that an audience through Simon's YouTube channel, a live creator conference and a feature film. We want a long-term relationship that starts with something creative.

01

Simon builds with Higgsfield on YouTube

A flagship YouTube video and five follow-ups in which Simon Squibb uses Higgsfield to make real ads for Dream's own products.

  • FlagshipOne dedicated video on Simon's YouTube channel, with an extended segment where he uses Higgsfield.
  • Follow-upsFive further videos, each featuring Higgsfield.
  • CommitmentSix months minimum.
  • PromotionShort clips cut from the videos, to send viewers to the full video.

What it looks like. Simon shows the process, start to finish. In one video he makes an ad for Dream, walks through its features and shows how Higgsfield produced the ad. In another he creates an ad for DreamBrew, another Dream product, entirely with AI. It is a working demonstration, not a passing mention, so viewers see exactly what they could build for their own business.

02

1BFS Activation

Higgsfield becomes headline creator sponsor of Dream's space at the 1 Billion Followers Summit (1BFS), with an online and an in-person activation.

What is 1BFS?The 1 Billion Followers Summit is a creator conference in Dubai, run by the UAE National Media Authority. The 2026 edition sold out with over 30,000 attendees, including more than 15,000 creators, and over 400 speakers are confirmed for 2027. The next one runs 8 to 10 January 2027.
What is 100 Dreams?Dream's programme with 1BFS. People apply online through Dream, and 100 winners each receive $10,000, flights to Dubai, a UAE company licence and a six-week online kickstart programme. Everyone who applies joins Dream and gets free help, whether they win or not.
  • SponsorshipHeadline creator sponsor of Dream's space at 1BFS, for the year.
  • OnlineA "Create with Higgsfield" button inside the 100 Dreams application journey on Dream: people join Dream, apply and pitch their dream. Applicants who would rather not film themselves can make a 60-second animated character clip about their dream instead.
  • In personA Higgsfield activation inside Dream's area at the summit, alongside the 100 Dreams gumball machine and the Dream Mentorship stand, where visitors generate their own animated character on the spot.
  • ReachThe 100 Dreams campaign aims for 1 billion views across social channels, led by Simon's following of 28 million.
  • ContentPosts about the activation, plus the YouTube integration from idea 01.
  • OptionalA performance deal (CPA: Higgsfield pays a fee for each new user we bring) on top of the sponsorship.

Why it works. Every applicant who would rather not be on camera becomes a Higgsfield creator and leaves with a clip made on your platform. It slots into the 100 Dreams programme we are already running, so the only additions are the button, the activation and the posts.

Go big
03

The Dream Movie

Simon Squibb's story as an animated feature film or series, made on Higgsfield and released together.

  • ConceptA feature-length film about Simon's story, or an animated series.
  • ProductionHiggsfield pays for the compute and produces the film using Higgsfield.
  • StoryDream writes the story and the script.
  • RightsHiggsfield gets the right to make the film with us, and a marketing story: look what you can build on Higgsfield.
  • DistributionHiggsfield can take it to cinemas and YouTube, and Simon promotes it on his channels.
  • TimelineWriting the script can take up to six months. Production resourcing is scoped once we agree the deal.

Why it works. It is advertising for Higgsfield in the form of a film: proof that the platform can carry a full story, as Higgsfield has shown with its own feature-length AI film. Think of it as building a DreamWorks together. Because ownership of AI-generated work is still unsettled, we would structure it as a paid production, with rights terms agreed together.
